How to read a performance claim

A curve without its drawdown, its sample size and its calculation method is decoration. The checklist below is the one a risk desk would apply to any track record, including its own.

The four questions

  • Is the equity curve broker-generated? Screenshots and spreadsheet curves prove nothing. Statements issued by the broker, or an API-derived series that can be reconciled to them, are the only credible source.
  • What is the maximum drawdown, and over what sample? A return figure is meaningless without the peak-to-trough loss that produced it and the number of trades behind it.
  • Are costs and slippage included? Gross figures flatter everything. Commission, exchange and platform fees, and realistic fills change conclusions.
  • Is the sample statistically meaningful? Thirty trades tell you almost nothing about a strategy. Sample-size scepticism is a professional habit, not negativity.
