STRUCTURE

How the desk is structured

The live floor is run by practising traders. That has two consequences worth being explicit about: sessions are described as the market is being read, including the occasions where nothing is done at all, and the reasoning is open to challenge by any member in the room.

Roles on the floor

  • Desk lead. Runs the pre-market map and the live narration during the London and New York sessions.
  • Risk reviewer. Audits member journals against the desk checklist and owns the rules on sizing and daily limits.
  • Allocation reviewer. Assesses readiness for larger size and manages the criteria for progression between programmes.

What the desk will not do

No member of the desk will place a trade for you, tell you what to buy, or accept compensation contingent on your trading results. The room is education and process review; execution and its outcomes remain entirely yours.
