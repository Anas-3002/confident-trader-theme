Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data this website collects, why it is collected, and how to have it removed.

EFFECTIVE FROM PUBLICATION

What we collect

This website collects only the information you choose to submit through its forms, plus the technical data any web server records in order to serve pages and defend against abuse.

Information you submit

When you complete an application, assessment request or contact form, we store the values you enter — typically your name, email address, country or timezone, experience level and message — together with the page you submitted from, a timestamp and the originating IP address. That record is kept so the desk can reply to you and so that abuse of the form can be investigated.

Technical data

Like every web server, ours logs requests (IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamp) for security and troubleshooting. These logs are retained for a limited operational period and are not used to build advertising profiles.

What we do not do

  • We do not run advertising or behavioural tracking pixels.
  • We do not sell, rent or share submitted data with third parties for marketing.
  • We do not use submitted data to train third-party models.

Cookies

This site sets no advertising cookies. WordPress sets a session cookie for logged-in administrators only. There is no public user login on this website, so ordinary visitors do not receive that cookie.

Your rights and removal

To request a copy of the information you have submitted, or its deletion, use the contact form and state that you are making a data request. Requests are actioned against the stored submission records, and you will receive confirmation once the record has been removed.

Third-party services

Fonts and icon glyphs used by this site are self-hosted from the same server that delivers the pages, so no visitor data is sent to a third-party font provider. Emails generated by the contact forms are delivered through the hosting provider's mail service.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the revised version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
