What changes after this program

The practical change is a shift in the questions you ask. Instead of "is this a bull flag", you start asking where the auction is being rejected, which side is trapped, and what level would prove you wrong. That reframing is what makes the rest of the curriculum usable.

Why it works across instruments

Auction logic is a description of how orders meet, not a property of one market. The same reasoning applies to index futures, FX, commodities and liquid equities, which is why members who trade ES can read crude or cable with the same framework after adjusting for session structure and typical range.

Prerequisites

You should already be able to place and manage an order on your platform, understand what a bid and an offer are, and have a basic grasp of contract sizes and margin. Everything else is taught from first principles: no prior order-flow experience is assumed.
