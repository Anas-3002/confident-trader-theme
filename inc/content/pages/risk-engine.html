METHODOLOGY

The only edge that compounds

Risk management is frequently sold as the boring part. It is in fact the only part with a guaranteed mathematical effect on your results. A trader with a marginal win rate and disciplined sizing outperforms a trader with a strong win rate who sizes emotionally.

Sizing from the stop, not from the feeling

The order of operations matters. Define the level that invalidates the idea, measure the distance to entry in ticks, decide the fraction of account equity you are willing to lose on this single idea, and only then calculate size. Sizing first and hunting for a stop afterwards inverts the whole process.

Correlation is hidden leverage

Four positions that all express the same macro view are one position at four times the size. The risk engine covers how to measure aggregated exposure across instruments and how to recognise when a portfolio is accidentally a single trade.

Behaviour is the binding constraint

Every rule in this program exists to be broken by a human under stress, so each rule is paired with a physical process: a written pre-trade checklist, a hard limit placed with the broker rather than in your head, and a review that happens before the next session opens.
