Risk statement

Hypothetical performance results have many inherent limitations. No representation is being made that any account will or is likely to achieve profits or losses similar to those shown. In fact, there are frequently sharp differences between hypothetical results and the actual results subsequently achieved by any particular trading programme.

1. Trading risk

Trading futures, foreign exchange, equities and other leveraged instruments involves substantial risk of loss and is not suitable for every investor. The valuation of such instruments may fluctuate, and you may lose more than your initial deposit. You should not trade with money you cannot afford to lose, and you should seek independent advice if you do not fully understand the risks involved.

2. Past performance

Past performance is not indicative of future results. Any performance figures, equity curves, ratios or win rates displayed on this website describe the desk methodology, are illustrative of it, and are not a representation of the results any individual will achieve. They are not audited client results and must not be relied upon as such.

3. Proprietary trading evaluations

Evaluation programmes offered by third-party proprietary trading firms are commercial products with their own rules, fees and failure conditions. Confident Trader is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or responsible for any such firm, and does not guarantee that any participant will pass an evaluation, receive funding, or retain a funded account. Any pass rate figure quoted by any provider should be treated with scepticism unless published with a stated methodology.

4. No advice

Nothing on this website is investment, legal, tax or accounting advice. Examples and illustrations are used for teaching purposes only. Confident Trader does not know your financial situation, objectives or risk tolerance, and cannot recommend a course of action for you.

5. Testimonials and illustrations

Journeys and quotes shown on this website are illustrative of the methodology and are not verified statements from named clients unless expressly labelled as such and accompanied by supporting documentation.

6. Technology and execution risk

Platform outages, connectivity failures, data-feed errors, slippage and liquidity gaps can cause losses that risk controls cannot prevent. No methodology removes operational risk, and no risk-management framework guarantees a particular outcome.

7. Regulatory note

Any reference to rules such as CFTC Rule 4.41 is provided for context and is not a claim that Confident Trader is registered with, supervised by, or endorsed by any regulator. If you are unsure whether trading is appropriate for you, consult a licensed professional in your jurisdiction.
